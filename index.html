How to Play Solitaire

Welcome to Solitaire (Klondike)! The main objective of the game is to move all cards to the four Foundation piles located at the top right of the board.

The Board Layout

  • Stock Pile (Top Left): Contains all the remaining face-down cards. Click this pile to draw cards.
  • Waste Pile (Next to Stock): Cards drawn from the stock pile will appear here face-up. You can play the top card of this pile.
  • Foundations (Top Right): The 4 designated slots for each suit. You must start building these with Aces, followed by 2, 3, and so on up to King.
  • Tableau (Bottom Columns): The 7 main columns where most of the gameplay happens.

Game Rules

  • Moving Cards in Tableau: You can place a card on top of another card in the tableau columns ONLY if it is exactly one rank lower and of the opposite color (e.g., placing a Red 6 on top of a Black 7).
  • Moving Stacks: You can move a full face-up stack of cards together if the bottom card of that moving stack forms a valid sequence with the target card.
  • Empty Columns: If a tableau column becomes empty, you can only move a King (or a stack starting with a King) into that empty space.
  • Building Foundations: Find the Aces and move them to the 4 top-right slots first. Then, continue building them up by suit (e.g., place the 2 of Hearts on the Ace of Hearts).
  • Recycling the Stock: If the stock pile runs out, click the empty slot to flip the waste pile over and recycle the cards.

Scoring System

  • Moving a card to the Foundation gives you +10 points.
  • Moving a card from the Waste to the Tableau gives you +5 points.
  • Revealing a face-down card in the Tableau gives you +5 points.
  • Note: Taking a card OUT of the Foundation back to the Tableau will deduct -10 points from your score.
