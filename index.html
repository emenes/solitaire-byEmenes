How to Play Solitaire

Welcome to Solitaire (Klondike)! The ultimate goal is to move all cards to the four Foundation piles located at the top right of the board. The game demands strategic planning and patience.

📍 The Board Layout

  • Stock Pile (Top Left): Contains all the remaining face-down cards. Click this pile to draw cards.
  • Waste Pile (Next to Stock): Cards drawn from the stock pile will appear here face-up. You can play the top card of this pile.
  • Foundations (Top Right): The 4 designated slots for each suit. You must start building these with Aces, followed by 2, 3, and so on up to King.
  • Tableau (Bottom Columns): The 7 main columns where the majority of your gameplay will take place.

🎮 Game Rules & Controls

  • Moving Cards (Single Click): You can simply Single Click a card to automatically move it to a valid position, or you can drag and drop it.
  • Invalid Moves: If a card cannot be moved when clicked, it will playfully shake to let you know the move is invalid.
  • Tableau Sequencing: Place a card on top of another in the tableau columns ONLY if it is exactly one rank lower and of the opposite color (e.g., Red 6 on top of a Black 7).
  • Moving Stacks: A full face-up stack can be moved together if the bottom card of the moving stack forms a valid sequence with the target card.
  • Empty Columns: If a tableau column becomes empty, you can only move a King (or a stack starting with a King) into that space.
  • Building Foundations: Find the Aces and move them to the 4 top-right slots first. Then continue building up by suit.
  • Recycling the Stock: When the stock pile runs out, click the empty slot to flip the waste pile over and recycle the cards.

🏆 Features (V8.4)

  • Dynamic Difficulty: The system now automatically assigns a randomized, optimized difficulty level each game to keep things challenging yet solvable!
  • Live Timer: Timer will start automatically upon your first move! Finish the game to record your time.
  • Auto Solve: When all face-down cards are completely revealed, a shiny ⭐ Auto Solve button will appear to automatically finish your game!
  • Reset & Undo: Rearrange cards to the initial state, or simply undo your latest move.
  • Auto Save: Game will continuously save via LocalStorage. You can resume anytime even after closing the browser!
